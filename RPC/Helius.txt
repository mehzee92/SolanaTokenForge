Extra Credits
You can purchase more credits if you exhaust your credits within a billing cycle. 

Crypto Plans:
You can purchase additional prepaid credits directly from the dashboard for crypto plans. 

Card Payment Plans:
For plans paid via card, you can enable autoscaling in the Project Settings on the dashboard. This automatically adjusts credits based on usage, ensuring you have enough resources as demand increases. This is charged at the end of your billing cycle. 

Note that renewing/extending your plan will not immediately renew credits; credits will refresh only at the start of the next billing cycle.

Credit rates vary depending on the plan. If you regularly incur overages, consider upgrading your plan.
